## Idea

The analogue of polynomial factorization of [[polynomial functions]] over the [[complex numbers]], but for entire complex analytic functions, which come with a countable number of roots instead of a finite number of roots for polynomials.

## Definition

Given a complex analytic function $f$ which is entire in the sense that the Taylor series expansion of $f$ converges on all of the complex numbers, the **Weierstrass factorization theorem** states that there exists a countable number of roots $z_n$ such that 

$$f(z) = \pi_{n \in \mathbb{N}} (z - z_n)$$
