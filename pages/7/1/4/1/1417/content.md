#  The Noninherent-Analytic Inner Philosophy:
  Of Nonexistence and Negligibles.
---

## Introduction

You are not a whole. You are not a partial. You are not a nothing. Your
presence is fleeting. I can make orders that make you lesser, I can make
orders where you dominate. But none of those exist in reality so they
are equally meaningless. You cannot be found and yet by inferential ways
your limits or your boundaries can be tested, confirmed, and validated.
You can, and all that makes you up, and must be bounded but the limits
of your interactions are beyond your earthly understanding, given as a
nonclosed body system containing mass you are contorted by the force of
unseen galaxies and your electrons are spun by untraceable
electromagnetic fields. You sit, stand, or lay down on an oblate
spheroid of heterogeneous material that travels through the universe at
a staggering 600 km/s. Yet you feel none of it, for if you did that
would surely mean the doom of all you know and love on Earth. You are a
blissful ignorant unaware of real dooms yet distracted by trivialities.
And the only thing epistemology can explain to you about yourself is a
philosophy made by other imperfect and fleeting things.

The most fundamental idea of your life, your existence, can be refuted
with one concept that sits outside all maths (known and countable). The
definition of existence as "the state of being, existing, or occurring;
beinghood" is an empty vessel of convenience. I won't bother defining
"be" as this word has so many meanings it is meaningless. Nonetheless, I
will show by inherency you are noninherent and that violates existence.
And can never be otherwise. And it's wrong.

Ideas turned into a mental object that people consider, modify, and
explain are mostly based on existence. Like the idea of gravity. Gravity
has a set of ideas, concepts, rules, math and so on that are all
packaged into the philosophy of gravity. But gravity doesn't "exist"
outside other forces known in the common vernacular as the "standard
model." But gravity is like every object we know it's an incomplete
without the rest of Kant's architectonic, it lacks what they don't
mention. What most people that taught you stuff never revealed is most
of the things we claim are true are probably true or are as true as we
can understand them, not ACTUALLY true because they don't have a way to
make it certain. That is the peril of all knowledge based on analytic *a
priori* facts, as Kant warned. This honest withholding of doubtful
validity comes from arrogance[^1]. It is prideful to assume YOUR ideas
are better or more complete than others without tribunal to proves them
without doubt. Few scientists have the willpower to defend in public
incomplete or fragile philosophies.

What I mean to propose is a set of ideas that can help you understand
all the others. It is based on the single most provable idea:
noninherent-existence. In the most basic presentation that just means
you or any thing relies on a host of other things to be. Without them
you can't be found. Without others those can't be found. *ad infinitum*.
Like you, any theory can then be made accepting there are still others,
perhaps not known, that undergird[^2] them.

Why is this important to you and your time? The ideas we base our life
on get stuck inside dogma[^3] and there are few attempts to correct any
philosophy. It is very hard to satisfy both sides of any philosophy: the
empirical wants to see a number, the qualitativists[^4] want you to
satisfy a dubious condition. Neither side is complete and ergo neither
side can be correct but still they rage at each other. The organizing
principles get swapped out, branded a new way, and then most of the
ideas are recycled. But the ideas can't solve reality without a new way
not more spin. The gap never closes because the debate isn't about being
right just comfortable. All philosophies are inevitably wrong but none
refuse to accept the other sides' valid criticisms. The defense of all
of them is religious not rationale. Religion favours emotions over true
reason. Belief is a copout for effort. Allow me to present all sides are
wrong, especially me. The only distinction is my humility is a feature
of my inner philosophy not a bug.

An answer to the existence question can be found within Charles Sanders
Pierce's[@sowa1995syntax] ideas assisted by Kant[@kant1908critique],
Wittgenstein[@Wittgenstein1922Tractatus; @Wittgenstein2010PhilInvestigations],
Marcus Aurelius[@aurelius2006meditations],
Sowa[@sowa1992logical; @sowa1995syntax; @sowa1998conceptual; @sowa2000knowledge],
and and many others faded into the distance.

Let me divert from the rigour of formalizing what noninherent-existence
means by just waxing poetic about you and what the "you" means. Let's
start from what you think you know.

Let me call you Ralph, for Ralph Wiggum, because when we peer into the
determinism of the universe and try to extend metaphysics we are all
little more than any bewildered 8 year old boy.

Ralph as a unit cannot be found. If I cut your arm off, are you still
called Ralph? Yes. So what did that arm mass have to do with you? Your
analytic network of things connected to others just suffered a
monumental loss of nodes, your arm now fallen, yet we can still find
you, Ralph. You exist but you aren't an entire graph, order, nor a
magnitude? How?

You drink a Coke, are you a greater Ralph? No. You aren't the sum of
your atoms, you aren't your mass with your piss or without it. Orders,
sets, and graphs can be used to describe you but each and everyone
cannot discover you. Nor can they explain nor encompass you, in any way,
day, or fashion. The exact makeup of Ralph by those existence axioms
varies instantaneously yet no one confuses which you is you. If you
lined up a billion variant Ralphs from time history and declared them
equivalent[^5] you'd be put in a math asylum[^6]. Like saying 4 = 6 = 9
= -1222. Then how can any of them be you? None of them were you because
you are a name applied as an ablation to many many other things.
Noninherent-existence doesn't rely on those axioms. Nor existence.

If your soul can escape when you die, why can't they jump out before
your car hits the wall? Linked and yet fatalistic? God has a dark
humour? Perhaps.

Ralph's arm lay cold on the bloody floor near his sky-facing eyes, so
where in the chalk outline does Ralph begin and where does Ralph end? In
time or space? Can't people subjectify each their own satisfactory
limits to Ralph? Yes. So which one is CORRECT? NONE. Ralph was never all
there just his pieces. And his soul flew off, or something. Doesn't that
extend him indefinitely? Does saintly Ralph exist without his soul, or
body without soul? How did that soul appear before you existed?

I can continue to present riddles and paradoxes *ad infinitum*. But I
feel my point is made. There is no philosophy: analytic or qualitative,
empirical or positivist, or even kooky Žižekian, that can approach the
description of even one "existent object". And that's not even touching
Lacan and the fact language is by it's own right a product of mental
illness[^7]. And therein lies the problem for life, we struggle confused
with missing meanings based on terrible models that philosophers are
mute to assist in taming. Or expanding. Or improving.

Math does only slightly better.

Mathematicians are the best philosophers on the planet but each one
diverges to an internecine war of formalities and conventions. And then
they forget what the point of it all was. The truth is that with all
formalities one can impose rigour one leaves entire galactic vistas of
reality uncovered. Something no alive philosopher can be guilty of
possessing. Nor demonstrating.

So how does one approach explaining you and what you are without endless
similes and paradoxes? One adopts the least restrictive mechanism.

Let me diverge for a second to make it clear I am a polymath, an
electrical engineer, a soldier, scientist, creative, malcontent, father.
I have worn many uniforms and forged into many kinds of battles. My mind
sits in no camp and defends no banner except pragmatism and *Vernunft
über alles*. I was a rabid adherent to the religion of mathematics,
until I saw just how implausible some of the conceited assumptions /
axioms / conventions are. I read Kant. I read Wittgenstein, early and
late. I felt defeated by the dogma I'd donned day after day like armour,
but then I realized it was merely a paradigm shift to a more pragmatic
way. I read Charles Sanders Pierce (CSP), and realized how similar he
trod the paths of Immanuel Kant. I'd gone in a circle. And then I knew
how I'd help. They were all right because each in his own way admitted
they were wrong. And so is every other philosopher. And every
philosopher will be wrong until Kant's architectonic is fully assembled.

Making clear where one's ideas begin and end makes it easy to construct
therefrom. It is the value why reason trumps belief, we all conduct
intellectual capitalism to pick and choose what works from what is on
offer.

This one is forged directly from metaphysics and is designed OUTSIDE of
any mathematical form. It doesn't represent what one would call a
philosophy - a pervasive system that admits of universal truths for this
and every world (the way old people would declare this and every
universe).

The basis of presentation in a rigorous form is the confinement of a
philosophical idea to a mathematical boundary.

It works at the level of a logic when you consider it like a painting:
given some outer symbols and markings you can see what the state of all
announced variables in the painting is and make any kind of reasoning
upon it. It works at the level of a calculus when you unbind the frame
to consider moving variables and spaces flowing under common boundaries.
You can use a microscope to see inner philosophies. This paper sets out
an inner[^8] philosophy[^9] that leverages Charles Sanders Pierce (CSP)
existential graphs (see Figure
[1](#fig:CSP-Existential-Graph){reference-type="ref"
reference="fig:CSP-Existential-Graph"}) by reimagining his diagrams with
an essentialized sign, a newly created negligible boundary, and
noninherency as the sign operator that colocates all in the universe.

## What you will learn {#subsec:What-you-willLearn}

What this paper teaches, more than disillusioning you about your present
knowledge, what you assumed or believed but were unarmoured to defend,
is what is the best way to present and critique knowledge. I want to
assure you that MOST of your knowledge should remain IN TACT. However,
as a difference to how you STRUCTURED that knowledge in your mind (your
hardware is your brain, your software is your mind) this paper makes it
clear that KNOWLEDGE is STRUCTURE and the way to adapt and adopt new
knowledge overtop your prior knowledge is to present and maintain your
knowledge in DIAGRAMS. The ideas are common, even in various
noncommensurate[^10] theories based on various axioms. What will change
if you accept this inner philosophy is you will make YOUR understanding
clear and others will be able to understand THEIRS better through your
knowledge. How revolutionary! This is entirely revolutionary to the
wasteful and nonproductive journal process. We aren't in the business of
making things people won't read because secretly they don't understand.
Or care.

What is different in the knowledge you possess, from one expert to
another on the same topic, is the MINOR changes or modifications from
one diagram to another. What is missing, what is misdefined, what is
connected one to another, and so on, which while many are the ONLY
difference between one person's model and the next. No language based
first order logic is as intuitive.
