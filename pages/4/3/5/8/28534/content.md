## Idea

The seventh [[stable homotopy group of spheres]] (the seventh [[stable stem]]) is the [[cyclic group]] of [[order of a group|order]] 240 (cf. [Toda 1961 p. 186](#Toda62)):

\[
  \label{QuaternionicHopfFibrationGeneratingpi3}
  \array{
    \pi_7^s &\simeq& \mathbb{Z}/240
    \\
    [h_{\mathbb{O}}] &\leftrightarrow& [1]
  }
\]

where the generator $[1] \in \mathbb{Z}/240$ is represented by (the [[suspensions]] of) the [[octonionic Hopf fibration]] $S^{15} \overset{h_{\mathbb{O}}}{\longrightarrow} S^{8}$ (cf.  Prop. \ref{OctonionicHopfFibrationIsGenerator} below).


## Properties

### Generator

\begin{proposition}
\label{OctonionicHopfFibrationIsGenerator}
  A generator of $\pi_7^s$, is represented by the [[suspension]] of the [[quaternionic Hopf fibration]].
\end{proposition}
One would hope this could be cited directly from classical literature. But [Toda 1962](#Toda62) makes the statement only for the [[p-primary group|2-primary]] projection, [Adams 1966](#Adams1966) leaves it at best implicit, and also the review in [Ravenel 2004 Thm. 1.13](#Ravenel2004) still does not say it fully explicitly. Here is how to stitch this information together: 
\begin{proof}
  From [Toda 1962 p. 186](#Toda62) we know that $\pi_7^s \simeq \mathbb{Z}_{/240}$. With this, [Adams 1966 Thm. 1.16](#Adams1966) implies that the [[J-homomorphism]] is [[surjective]] onto $\pi_7^s$. (Use here that, in Adams's notation, $s = 2$, and then that the [[Bernoulli number]] $B_{2s} = B_4 = -1/30$ so that $B_{2s}/4s = -1/240$.) Finally, results by Adams and Quillen imply --- and [Ravenel 2004 Thm. 1.13](#Ravenel2004) makes explicit --- that the [[image]] of the [[J-homomorphism]] in degree 7 is generated by the ([[suspensions]] $\sigma$ of) the [[octonionic Hopf fibration]].
\end{proof}

See also [Randal-Williams 2025 p. 1991 (20), ftn 5](#Randal-Williams2025).
