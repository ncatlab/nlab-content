## Statement

For two [[2-spheres]] with respective radii $R$ and $r$ (with $R\gg r$) as well as distance $d$ between their centers, a three-dimensional [[simplex]] fits inbetween them (meaning it's fully inside the [[sphere]] with radius $R$ and fully outside the [[sphere]] with radius $r$) if and only if:
$$
d^2
\leq (R-r)(R+3r)
=R^2+2Rr-3r^2
$$
The [[Grace-Danielsson inequality]] is part of three results convering the case in different dimensions:

* $n=2$: [[Euler's theorem in geometry]]
* $n=3$: [[Grace-Danielsson inequality]]
* $n\geq 4$: [[Egan-Drozdov inequality]]

## History

The Grace-Danielsson inequality was independently proved by [[John Hilton Grace]] in [Grace 18](#Grace18) and [[Gösta Danielsson]] in [Danielsson 52](#Danielsson52). A connection to [[quantum information theory]] was described in [Milne 14](#Milne14).
