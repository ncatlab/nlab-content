## Definition

Let $C$ be a [[coherent category]]. Its category of filters, denoted by $\Lambda C$ is the category whose objects are pairs $(A,F)$, where $A\in C$ and $F\subseteq \sub_C(A)$ is a filter.  A morphism $(A,F)\to(B,G)$ in $\Lambda C$ is a germ $[\alpha]$ of local continuous maps, where a local continuous map $\alpha:(A,F)\to(B,G)$ is a morphism $\alpha:U\to B$ in $C$, where $U\in F$, such that for all $V\in G$, we have $\alpha^*(V)\in F$. Two such maps $\alpha_1:U_1\to B$ and $\alpha_2:U_2\to B$ are equivalent iff there exist $U\in F$ with $U\leq U_1\wedge U_2$ and $\alpha_1 |_U = \alpha_2|_U$.

The **topos of types** of $C$, denoted by $\mathbf{T}(C)$, is [[sheaf topos]] $sh(\tau C, J_p)$, where $\tau C$ is the full subcategory of $\Lambda C$ consisting of all pairs $(A,\rho)$ where $\rho\subseteq\sub_C(A)$ is a [[prime filter]], and $J_p$ is the topology induced on $\tau C$ by the [[coherent topology]] on $\Lambda C$.

 

This definition first appeared in [Makkai](#Makkai).

In particular, we can describe the topology $J_p$ as the topology generated by the singleton covers, i.e. a sieve $S$ on $(A,\rho)$ is covering in (\tau C, J_p) if and only if it contains an arrow $[\alpha]:(B,\sigma)\to(A,\rho)$ such that $\exists_{[\alpha]}\sigma = \rho$, or more explicitly $$\rho = \{V\in \sub_\C(A) : \alpha^*(V) \in \sigma\}.$ For more details see ([Coumans, Section 5](#Coumans))

**Note:** The word "types" here has nothing to do with [[type theory]].  Instead, refers to the notion of [[type (in model theory)|type in model theory]].

## Properties

### In terms of the coherent hyperdoctrine

Let $C$ be a [[coherent category]]. The [[canonical extension]] $\mathcal{S}_C^\delta$ of its [[coherent hyperdoctrine]] $\mathcal{S}_C$  is naturally an [[internal locale]] in the [[presheaf topos]] $[C^{op}, Set]$ (as well as in the [[sheaf topos]] $Sh_{coh}(C)$ for the [[coherent topology]] on $C$).

The topos of types is equivalent to the topos of [[internal sheaves]] over this [[internal locale]].

$$
  \mathbf{T}(C) 
  \simeq
  Sh(\mathcal{S}_C^\delta)  
  \,.
$$

This is due to ([Coumans, theorem 25](#Coumans)).

In particular this implies that there is a [[localic geometric morphism]]

$$
 \mathbf{T}(C) \to Sh_{coh}(C)
 \,.
$$

Let $Mod(C) \simeq Func_{coh}(C,Set)$ be the category of [[models]] of the [[coherent theory]] given by $C$ in [[Set]]. 

There is a [[full subcategory]] $\mathcal{K} \hookrightarrow Mod(C)$ such that the [[Yoneda extension]] of the [[evaluation map]] $ev : C \to Func(\mathcal{K}, Set)$ is the [[inverse image]] of a [[geometric morphism]]

$$
  \phi_{ev} : Set^{\mathcal{K}} \to Sh_{coh}(C)
  \,.
$$

The [[(hyperconnected, localic) factorization system|hyperconnected / localic factorization]] of this morphism is through the topos of types of $C$:

$$
  \phi_{ev}
   :
  Set^{\mathcal{K}}
  \stackrel{hyperconn.}{\to}
  \mathbf{T}(C)
  \stackrel{localic}{\to}  
  Sh_{coh}(C)
  \,.
$$

This is ([Coumans, theorem 39](#Coumans)).
