## Statement

If for a triangle the radius of its circumsphere is $R$, the radius of its insphere is $r$ and the distances of the centers of both spheres is $d$, then these are related by:
$$
d^2
=R(R+2r).
$$
[[Euler's theorem in geometry]] can be generalized to higher dimensions, but then becomes an inequality:

* $n=2$: [[Euler's theorem in geometry]]
* $n=3$: [[Grace-Danielsson inequality]]
* $n\geq 4$: [[Egan-Drozdov inequality]]

## History

The Euler's theorem in geometry was independently proved by [[William Chapple]] in [Chapple 46](#Chapple46) and [[Leonhard Euler]] in 1765.
