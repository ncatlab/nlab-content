## Idea

A weak theory of arithmetic, Presburger arithmetic is notable for being [[decidable]] and thus is most commonly used to define the universe levels of the [[universe hierarchy]] in [[dependent type theories]] such as [[Rocq]] or [[Lean]]. 

However, because Presburger arithmetic is not a second-order theory, it admits non-standard models; the [[natural numbers]] are only the [[initial object|initial]] model of Presburger arithmetic.
