## Idea

A weak [[theory]] of [[arithmetic]], *Presburger arithmetic* is notable for being [[decidable]] and thus is most commonly used to define the universe levels of the [[universe hierarchy]] in [[dependent type theories]] such as used in the [[proof assistants]] [[Rocq]] or [[Lean]]. 

However, because Presburger arithmetic is not a [[second-order logic|second-order]] theory, it admits non-standard models; the [[natural numbers]] are only the [[initial object|initial]] model of Presburger arithmetic.
