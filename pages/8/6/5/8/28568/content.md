## Idea

A [[large cardinal]] stronger than [[Berkeley cardinals]] that is inconsistent with [[ZFC]]. In order to remove the inconsistency, one can either choose to remove the [[axiom of choice]] to get [[ZF]] + a club Berkeley cardinal, or one can choose to remove the [[axiom of replacement]] and restrict to the [[axiom of bounded separation]] to get [[BZC]] + a club Berkeley cardinal. 

## Definition

### In ZF

In [[ZF]], a [[cardinal]] $\delta$ is a club Berkeley cardinal if it is regular and, for every club set (closed and unbounded set) $C \subseteq \delta$ and every [[transitive set]] $M$ containing $\delta$, there exists a non-trivial elementary embedding $j: M \to M$ such that its critical point lands exactly inside that club: $\mathrm{crit}(j) \in C$. 

### In BZC

In BZC, the definition of a club Berkeley cardinal is the same as the definition of a club Berkeley cardinal in ZF. However, without the [[axiom of replacement]], a club Berkeley cardinal is a much weaker large cardinal axiom; like [[Reinhardt cardinals]] and [[Berkeley cardinals]], club Berkeley cardinals still cannot even construct the large cardinal $\aleph_\omega$ in the absence of replacement. Furthermore, unlike [[ZF]], it is consistent to have a club Berkeley cardinal and [[V = L]] or [[V = Ultimate L]] in [[BZC]].
