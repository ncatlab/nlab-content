## Idea

In [[quantum computing]] and [[quantum information theory]], an *entangling gate* is a [[quantum gate]] on a [[tensor product of Hilbert spaces|tensor product]] [[finite dimensional Hilbert space]] that can send tensor product [[quantum states]] to [[quantum entanglement|entangled]] [[quantum states]].

## Properties

### For universality

[Bremner et al. 2002](#BremnerEtAl2002) showed that adjoining any entangling 2-[[qbit]] gate to the set of 1-[[qbit]] gates yields a [universal gate set](quantum+logic+gate#UniversalGateSets).
