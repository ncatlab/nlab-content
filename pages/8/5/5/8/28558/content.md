## Definition 

A __left Bruck loop__ is a left [[Bol loop]] $(K,\cdot)$ satisfying the automorphic inverse property
$$
(x y)^{-1} = x^{-1} y^{-1}
$$

Alternative terminologies: some authors say (left) K-loop or left gyrocommutative [[gyrogroup]].
