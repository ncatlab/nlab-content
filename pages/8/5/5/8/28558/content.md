## Definition 

\begin{definition}
A __left Bruck loop__ is a left [[Bol loop]] $(K,\cdot)$ satisfying the automorphic inverse property
$$
  (x y)^{-1} = x^{-1} y^{-1}
  \mathrlap{\,.}
$$
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
(Alternative terminologies)
Some authors say (left) *K-loop* or *left gyrocommutative [[gyrogroup]]*.
\end{remark}
