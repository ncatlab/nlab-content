**Type IIA Borcherds Algebra from Hypothesis H**

Abstract: The [[Whitehead L-infinity algebra|real Whitehead $L_\infty$-algebra]] $\mathfrak{l}(\text{-})$ of a [[classifying space]] $\mathcal{A}$ of [[brane]] [[charges]] in [[nonabelian cohomology]] characterizes the [[Bianchi identities]] of the [[flux densities]] sourced by these branes. For instance, $\mathfrak{l}(S^4)$ is the [M-theory gauge algebra](/nlab/show/supergravity+C-field#ReferencesCFieldGaugeAlgebra), whence *[[Hypothesis H]]* posits that [[4-sphere|$S^4$]] is the [[classifying space]] for [[M-brane]] [[charges]]. Seen after [[duality between M-theory and type IIA string theory|M-circle reduction to 10D]] these are [equivalently classified](/nlab/show/cyclic+loop+space#TheExtCycAdjunction) by the *[[cyclic loop space]]* $\mathrm{Cyc} S^4$ of M-circle images in $S^4$. Its subspace of *small* M-circles, $Cyc_1 S^4$, [[schreiber:K from M|is a nonabelian enhancement]] of [[D-brane charge quantization in K-theory|twisted K-theory]], and $\mathfrak{l}(Cyc_1 S^4)$ is the [[10D type IIA supergravity|IIA gauge algebra]] characterizing all NS/RR-flux Bianchis, including $F_8$ (that does not descend from 11D) and $H_7$ (which is missed by twisted K-theory). This [[schreiber:K from M|was shown recently]]. 

In this vein we identify here a further *minimal* subspace, $MCyc_1 S^4$, of small M-circle images in $S^4$, and show that $\mathfrak{l}\big(MCyc_1 S^4\big)$ is the *[positive type-IIA Borcherds algebra](/nlab/show/Borcherds+algebra#ForTypeIIA)*. This characterizes in addition the dual [[dilaton]] flux $H_9$, and beyond that an infinite tower of higher generators that thereby control the [[higher global symmetries]] of [[10D type IIA supergravity|type IIA SuGra]], when [[geometry of physics -- flux quantization|globally completed]] according to [[Hypothesis H]].

Finally we show that $MCyc S^4$ is equivalently a classifying space for [[cobordism classes]] of [[probe brane|probe]] [[D6-branes|6-branes]], whence we equivalently obtain a global Borcherds completion of [[10D type IIA supergravity|type IIA]] in ([[differential nonabelian cohomology|unstable]]) [[differential cobordism cohomology|differential]] [[cobordism cohomology]].
