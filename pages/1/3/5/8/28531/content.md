Jeffrey Bregfalk is a Research Fellow at the University of Barcelona. He is research is on the interactions between [[set theory]] and [[algebraic topology]], broadly understood.

In particular, he has worked on how the vanishing of the [[derived functors]] of the [[limit]] functor (cf. *[[lim^1]]*) depends on the [[underlying]] set theory.  This interacts with the behaviour of [[strong homology]] and with results on [[condensed sets]].
