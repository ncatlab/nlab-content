Jeffrey Bregfalk is a Research Fellow at the University of Barcelona. He is research is on the interactions between set theory and algebraic topology, broadly understood.

In particular, he has worked on how the vanishing of the derived functors of the limit functor depends on the set theory in which one is working.  This also interacts with the behaviour of [[strong homology]].  This in turn interacts with results on [[condensed sets]].
