## Idea

In [[physics]]/[[quantum field theory]], the *completeness hypothesis* due to [Polchinski 2004](#Polchinski2004) is a statement about the applicability of ([[Dirac charge quantization|Dirac]] [[geometry of physics -- flux quantization|charge quantization]]): which says that ([p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0304042#page=3)):

> "...for every [[gauge field]] there will exist electric and magnetic [[monopole|sources]]..."

or in more detail:

> "In any [[GUT|fully unified theory]], for every gauge field there will exist electric and magnetic sources with the minimum relative [[Dirac charge quantization|Dirac quantum]] $n = 1$",

where the minimality clause is further explained to mean that:

> "more precisely, the [[lattice (discrete subgroup)|lattice]] of electric and magnetic [[charges]] is maximal."

The term "completeness hypothesis" for this statement appears in [Banks & Seiberg 2011 p. 1](#BanksSeiberg2011), now counted as a "[[swampland conjecture]]" (cf. [Harlow & Ooguri 2021 p. 1](#HarlowOoguri2021)). A precise formulation appears in [SS 2025 Rem. 4.5 (v)](#SS2025).
