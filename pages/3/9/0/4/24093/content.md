There are multiple notions of **premetrics** and **premetric spaces** in the mathematical literature:

* [[premetric space (Booij)]]

* [[premetric space (Gilbert)]]

* [[premetric space (Richman)]]

* [[premetric locale]]

One can also have a distinction between whether one uses the positive rational numbers or the positive real numbers in the premetrics as defined by Booij and by Gilbert, leading to *rational premetric spaces* and *real premetric spaces*.
