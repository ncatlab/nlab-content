#Definition#

A [[category]] $C$ is **cofinally small** if there is a [[small category]] $C_0$ and a [[cofinal functor]] $C_0 \to C$.

Of course, if we choose the terminology of *final functors*, this property is called *finally small*.

If $C$ is [[locally small]], then $C$ is cofinally small if and only if it has a small cofinal [[full subcategory]] (i.e. the inclusion functor is cofinal).
