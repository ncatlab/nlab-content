## Statement

For two $n-1$-[[spheres]] with respective radii $R$ and $r$ (with $R\gg r$) as well as distance $d$ between their centers, a $n$-dimensional [[simplex]] fits inbetween them (meaning it's fully inside the [[sphere]] with radius $R$ and fully outside the [[sphere]] with radius $r$) if and only if: 
$$
d^2
\leq(R-(n-2)r)(R+nr)
=R^2+2Rr-(n-2)nr^2.
$$
The [[Egan-Drozdov inequality]] is part of three results convering the case in different dimensions:

* $n=2$: [[Euler's theorem in geometry]]
* $n=3$: [[Grace-Danielsson inequality]]
* $n\geq 4$: [[Egan-Drozdov inequality]]

## History

[[Greg Egan]] conjectured the above result in [Baez 14](#Baez14). In comments lost during the website being moved, he proceeded to show the inequality was sufficient in 2018. [[Sergei Drozdov]] showed the inequality was also necessary in [Drozdov 25](#Drozdov25).
