## Idea

The axiom of constructibility states that the [[constructible universe]] $L$ and the [[von Neumann universe]] $V$ are identical to each other. 

## Properties

### In ZFC

In ZFC, the axiom of constructibility is inconsistent with [[measurable cardinals]]. 

### In BZC

In [[BZC]], the axiom of constructibility is consistent with large cardinal axioms such as [[Reinhardt cardinals]], [[Berkeley cardinals]], [[club Berkeley cardinals]], and [[limit club Berkeley cardinals]]. The lack of the [[axiom of replacement]] in [[BZC]] prevents the usual proofs of Kunan's inconsistency of these large cardinals with ZFC, with or without the axiom of constructibility, from working. 

However, the axiom of constructibility is still inconsistent with [[measurable cardinals]] in BZC. The thing here is that without replacement, [[Reinhardt cardinals]], [[Berkeley cardinals]], [[club Berkeley cardinals]], and [[limit club Berkeley cardinals]] are relatively weak: they do not imply the existence of $\aleph_\omega$, let alone [[measurable cardinals]].
