## Idea

The axiom of constructibility in [[set theory]] states that the [[constructible universe]] $L$ and the [[von Neumann universe]] $V$ are identical to each other. 

In the absence of the [[axiom of replacement]], one can't actually construct the [[constructible universe]] $L$, so one has to postulate the [[constructible universe]] $L$ in the [[set theory]] via an [[axiom schema]] before formulating the axiom of constructibility. 

## Properties

In [[IZF]], the axiom of constructibility does not imply the [[axiom of choice]] or [[excluded middle]]. 

In [[ZFC]] and [[IZF]], the axiom of constructibility is inconsistent with [[measurable cardinals]]. 

The axiom of constructibility in [[ZFC]] is equivalent to the statement that every [[exact functor]] $F: Set \to Set$ is [[natural isomorphism|naturally isomorphic]] to the [[identity functor]] on the [[category of sets]]. However, this equivalence fails in [[IZF]].
