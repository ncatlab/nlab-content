## Idea

An **identifier** is a particular kind of [[conical limit|conical]] [[2-limit]] in a [[2-category]], which universally renders a 2-cell between a [[parallel pair]] of 1-cells the equality 2-arrow. Its diagram shape is a single 2-arrow $\alpha\colon f \Rightarrow g$, for $f,g\colon X\to Y$ a pair of 1-arrows.
