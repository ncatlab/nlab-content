## Definition

A [[representation]] is called:

1. _reducible_ if it is *not [[irreducible representation|irreducible]]*, hence if it contains a non-[[zero object|zero]] [[subrepresentation]];

2. *completely reducible* if it is a [[direct sum]] of [[irreducible representations]].
