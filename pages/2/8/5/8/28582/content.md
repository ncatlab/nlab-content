## Idea

Given:

1. a [[complex numbers|complex]] [[Hilbert space]] $\mathcal{H}$, with [[projective space]] $P(\mathcal{H})$ of [[pure quantum states]], 

2. a smooth family $\Psi$ of [[pure quantum states]], hence a [[smooth map]] $X \overset{\Psi}{\longrightarrow} P(\mathcal{H})$ from a [[smooth manifold]] $X$,

then the *quantum metric* on this family is the [[pullback of differential forms|pullback]] of the [[Fubini-Study metric]] to $X$.

In [[quantum information theory]] this is also called the *quantum Fisher information metric* or *quantum information metric*.
