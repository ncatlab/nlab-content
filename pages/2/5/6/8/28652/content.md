## Idea

A *topological quantum gate* is a [[quantum gate]] in [[topological quantum computation]].

The ubiquituous paradigm for topological quantum gates are [[braiding]] operations of [[worldlines]] of [[anyons]] in [[topological order|topologically ordered]] [[quantum materials]], which by [[adiabatic quantum transport]] induce [[unitary operator]] on the [[Hilbert space]] of [[ground states]] of the system.

But, in principle, topological quantum gates could be more general, see at *[[topological order]]* the section *[A defintion and its examples](topological+order#DefinitionAndExamples)*.
