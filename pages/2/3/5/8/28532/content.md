## Idea

A generalization of the notion of [[topological spaces]] with [[proper maps]] between them, resulting in a [[bicomplete category]].

## Definition

An __exterior space__ is a [[topological space]] equipped with an _externology_: a collection of [[open subsets]] (known as __exterior sets__) that is closed under finite [[intersection]] and passage to open [[supersets]].

A __morphism of exterior spaces__ is a [[continuous map]] such that the [[preimage]] of an exterior set is exterior.

## Properties

The [[category]] of exterior spaces is [[complete category|complete]] and [[cocomplete category|cocomplete]].

The category of topological spaces and [[proper maps]] [[full subcategory|embeds fully faithfully]] into exterior spaces.

Thus, exterior spaces form a natural categorical setting for [[proper homotopy theory]].
