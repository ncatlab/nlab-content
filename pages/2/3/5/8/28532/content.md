## Idea

A generalization of the notion of spaces and [[proper maps]] between them, resulting in a bicomplete category.

## Definition

An __exterior space__ is a [[topological space]] equipped with an _externology_: a collection of open sets (known as __exterior sets__) that is closed under finite intersection and passage to open supersets.

A __morphism of exterior spaces__ is a continuous map such that the preimage of an exterior set is exterior.

## Properties

The category of exterior spaces is complete and cocomplete.

The category of topological spaces and [[proper maps]] embeds fully faithfully into exterior spaces.

Thus, exterior spaces form a natural categorical setting for [[proper homotopy theory]].
